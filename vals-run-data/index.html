Score variance and statistical power

  • 490 metadata-matched configurations have at least three runs; 411 remain after excluding known task-set or contract conflicts and requiring three valid outcomes.
  • The binomial reference is shown only when the benchmark's primary task outcome is binary. Continuous scores use empirical repeated-run variance instead.
  • Sample SDs receive the normal-theory c₄ small-sample correction; uncertainty intervals remain wide for configurations with three or four runs.
  • For LLM-judged scores, the available archive generally cannot separate model-execution variance from judge variance.
  • Across 263 comparable binary-score configurations, the median observed/reference SD ratio is 0.76 and the p90 is 2.68.
  • Across 15 fitted benchmarks, actual median repetition and the repetitions required for a 10 percentage-point comparison have Spearman correlation -0.05. This is descriptive evidence from 15 benchmark estimates.

Findings

  • The archive contains many benchmark and model combinations, but repetition is thin: 36.5% of benchmark–model pairs have one finished run and 51.3% have at most two.
